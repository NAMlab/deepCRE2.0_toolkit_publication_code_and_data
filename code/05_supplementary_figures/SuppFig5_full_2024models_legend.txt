Supplementary Figure 5, rebuilt in full, 2024models models (20261109 rerun). ZmUBI1 is a donor added in this rerun and sits in sequence as panel g), so the published panels g-n appear here as h-o.

a) Segmentation of the 3020 bp deepCRE input window; gUR and gTUR are the segments replaced by donor promoter sequence. Donor coordinates were checked against the genes they come from: the AtACT1 donor is identical to window positions 1-1500 of AT2G37620 extracted by the toolkit itself (0 mismatches), the CaMV 35S donor is the 1500 bp immediately upstream of the Hyg(R) start in pGZ14.1021 (GenBank KP100426, exact), and for every donor gURgTUR[1:1000] equals its gUR file and gURgTUR[1001:1500] its gTUR file.
b-g) Species run left to right by relatedness from Arabidopsis thaliana: Brassicaceae, Caryophyllales, asterids, then the grasses. C. sativa, B. vulgaris and O. sativa are added in this rerun; the published figure had only A. thaliana, S. lycopersicum, S. bicolor and Z. mays. Nine Arabidopsis thaliana genes per bar (n = 9); bars are the mean change in predicted probability of high expression, error bars the standard deviation, points individual genes. Two-sided one-sample t-test of the nine per-gene changes against zero.
g) ZmUBI1 gTUR: the maize polyubiquitin-1 locus of Zea mays GCF_902167145.1, NC_050100.1 minus strand; the gTUR is the first 500 bp of the transcript, i.e. the 5' UTR exon plus the start of intron 1. Its gUR and gUR+gTUR swaps are in SuppFig5_swaps_allmodels_bars_*.png.
h, j, l) Predicted probability of MEF1 (AT5G52630) natively and after the donor gTUR swap. i, k, m) DeepSHAP contribution scores summed over the four bases, window positions 950-1560; the dotted line is the gUR/gTUR boundary and the mustard band the aligned high contribution region, the 46 bp window of largest summed |contribution| inside that donor's gTUR, each shown with the model named in the panel title. Regions: AtACT1 1269-1314 (2024 Zea mays (leaf)), OsACT1 1147-1192 (2024 Zea mays (leaf)), PpACT1 1386-1431 (2024 Zea mays (leaf)).
n) The published OsACT1/AtACT1/PpACT1 alignment (Supplementary file 3, 339 columns; each donor's ungapped 250 bp occupies window positions 1151-1400 of its own gTUR), shown over the block 1185-1230. A dash is a gap. Bold, highlighted columns are identical in all three donors: 16 of the 40 such columns in the whole alignment fall in this 46 bp block, against 7.4 expected (one-sided binomial p = 0.00119), and no 46 bp window of the aligned region holds more. The bar track is the DeepSHAP contribution score of the OsACT1 gTUR swap under 2024 Zea mays (leaf), scaled to its own maximum.
o) Only window positions 1185-1230 of the OsACT1 gTUR — the 46 bp block of panel n — are pasted into the same positions of each test gene's own window; everything else stays native, so the change is attributable to those 46 bases. Three constructs per gene: the fragment intact, the fragment with all 16 sites conserved across OsACT1/AtACT1/PpACT1 transverted (A<->C, G<->T). Nine genes; the leaf and the root model named below. Brackets give the two-sided paired t-test of the intact fragment against the conserved-site degeneration. A control transverting the same number of NON-conserved sites of the same fragment (mean of 20 seeded draws per gene; drawn sites in fragment_20261109_control_sites.tsv) was run alongside and is reported below but no longer plotted.
